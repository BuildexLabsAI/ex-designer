- 2026-10-28: The brand book step (6b) follows the proposal Esko approved after the research in `docs/research/brand-book-practice.md`: 9 core chapters and 5 optional ones by trigger, a source ledger whose every source a script resolves (a quote must be on the page as read), two checkpoints, and the kit (`brand/`) kept apart from the internal work folder (`brand-work/`). It replaces the brand-skill phases and the 17-chapter shape of 2026-10-27, because the professional standard is usable and exact rather than long, purpose and values cannot be read off a website, and the ledger and the owner's answers must never reach makers.
- 2026-10-27: The design system page holds the system only (key colours per brand, every used colour by role, fonts, type sizes, corners, readable text pairs); values outside the tokens, unused tokens and the coverage share go to the audit report, and the owner's questions and the gaps are asked in chat one at a time (`tokens.py questions`), because Esko found the page full of audit data that a maintainer does not need in the reference he keeps open. A brand's font is the font-family its own theme selector sets, else its font-sans token, so a theme that sets its font directly (a theme class on the pilot site) is not shown in the fallback Arial.
- 2026-10-27: The audit report is separate from the design system and shows each page as one full-page screenshot with numbered markers next to one list, which opens on "Fix these first" and switches to the severe, moderate or minor findings; markers and list share numbers, a finding with no place on the page gets a grey number, and colour references stay in the design system.
